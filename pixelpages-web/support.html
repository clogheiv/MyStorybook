Common help topics

Creating a child profile

Add a child profile to personalize story text, artwork, and reading progress for that reader.

Choosing a story

Open the complete story list to browse available PixelPages stories and choose one for the active child profile.

Continue Reading

PixelPages saves reading progress on the device so a story can pick up where it left off.

Offline artwork

Current story artwork is bundled with the app so illustrated stories can be enjoyed without downloading images during reading.

iPhone/iPad reader rotation

The reader is designed for a comfortable storybook layout. If the app asks you to rotate your device, turn it sideways and wait for the story to open.
