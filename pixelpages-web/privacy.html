Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

PixelPages and your family's privacy

PixelPages is a children's storybook app made for family reading. This policy explains how the current PixelPages MVP handles information.

Local child profiles

Child profiles are stored locally on the device. PixelPages uses those profiles to personalize names, character style, and reading progress.

Local reading progress

Reading progress is stored locally on the device so the app can offer Continue Reading and profile-specific progress.

No account required

The current app does not require account creation. PixelPages does not sell personal data and does not use ads.

Children's information

PixelPages does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The current app is designed around local profiles and bundled story content.

Story artwork

Current story artwork is bundled with the app and used locally for the story reader experience.

Future features

If future PixelPages features add cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other services, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@pixelpagesapp.com.
